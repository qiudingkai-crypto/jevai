FAQ

Billing without surprises

One token allowance across all Jev tools.

What is the Jev AI platform?+

Jev AI brings TypeSafe’s Jev models into one workspace for playground, batch processing, and API access. The platform is independently operated, with subscriptions, token balances, and customer support managed by Jev AI.

How is paid usage measured?+

Actual input tokens are counted across playground, batch, API and AI judge generation. Output tokens are free. There is no per-request charge for paid usage.

How do free gifts work?+

Welcome and daily check-in gifts are used first, one per request. Once gifts are used up, requests use your paid token allowance.

How does annual billing work?+

Pay once per year at 50% off twelve monthly payments. The full year of tokens is added upfront.

Do unused tokens expire?+

Unused paid tokens roll over. One-time token packs do not require a subscription.

What happens if a request fails?+

Failed requests are not charged. Any temporary balance hold is released automatically.

Can I cancel renewal?+

Yes. Manage Stripe or PayPal subscriptions from your account.
